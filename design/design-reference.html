Marine insurance is a broad field. The right questions depend on what is at risk, who holds the insured interest and where responsibilities begin and end.

The insured interest

Describe what needs protection and your relationship to the goods, vessel or operation.

Movement and geography

Explain the route, transport stages, storage periods or operating area.

Values and responsibilities

Set out the values and contractual allocation of risk for discussion.

Existing protection

Identify any current policy and the points you want to clarify.
