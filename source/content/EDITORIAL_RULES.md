# Content rendering rules
Render all supplied paragraphs without truncation, except card excerpts. Use exactly one H1: strip the first Markdown H1 from article/legal body when the page hero already renders it. Article frontmatter is kept in article-index.json, not duplicated in Markdown. Do not assign the generation date as publication or professional review date. Preview byline: “Bright Fortune · Draft guide”; publication/review dates omitted until approved values exist. For public release, company may adopt organisation authorship; do not invent an individual professional reviewer.

The core service range is confirmed by the company's published site, not independently revalidated against current underwriting arrangements. Legal and insurance material remains company-review draft. Facts such as founder and dates have source support. Marketing process language represents the proposed site proposition and requires business review. The adjective “independent” is intentionally absent from the headline/meta default pending current company approval of that positioning.

BMS gaps are addressed through module and topic coverage, not copied paragraphs. Service pages contain unique introductions, areas to consider, practical information, FAQs, related services and guides. Marine educational discussion does not imply availability of cargo/hull/P&I products. Optional subclass copy is in docs/MARINE_REVIEW_APPENDIX.md and not in route content.

Do not add UK law references, BMS's London office, global staff, claims payments, insurer capacity, market-leading statements, case studies, invented office/staff portraits, fake testimonials or mock reviews. Do not show obsolete membership/regulatory language from the old site as current. Do not use ops@bright-fortune.com: that hyphenated domain is a different business.
