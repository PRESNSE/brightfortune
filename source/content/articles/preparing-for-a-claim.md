# Preparing for an Insurance Claim

A practical checklist for keeping the policy, event details and supporting information together.

## Follow the policy’s reporting instructions
If a loss or insured event has occurred, locate the relevant policy and follow its notification and assistance requirements. Some policies specify particular channels, information or time limits. A general website enquiry should not be assumed to satisfy those requirements.

If anyone is in immediate danger, use the appropriate emergency services. For emergency assistance under a policy, use the insurer’s designated instructions. Bright Fortune’s general enquiry form is not an emergency service.

## Record the basic facts
Write down the date, location and a brief description of what happened while the details are available. Keep the policy number, policyholder name and contact details together. If you have already contacted the insurer, record the reference and the person or team you dealt with.

Distinguish information you know from estimates or matters you are still checking. If a detail later changes, keep a clear record of the correction. Accurate information is more useful than attempting to make the first account appear complete.

## Retain relevant supporting material
Documents requested will depend on the policy and event. Relevant receipts, correspondence, photographs or reports may assist, but do not put yourself at risk to obtain them. Ask the insurer or broker what should be retained and what can be provided later.

Keep originals where appropriate and make an organised record of copies supplied. Follow any policy requirements concerning mitigation, repairs, admissions or agreements; seek clarification if you are unsure before taking steps that could affect the claim.

## Initial contact checklist
- Policy or certificate number, if available.
- Policyholder name and a way to contact you.
- Date, location and a short description of the event.
- Any insurer or assistance reference already received.
- Any stated reporting deadline or urgent request.
- A list of supporting documents available.

Do not submit identity documents, medical records, bank details or a full claim file through the public enquiry form. Ask for the appropriate document channel. Keeping the first message brief can help establish the right next step without unnecessary disclosure.

## Understand the broker’s and insurer’s roles
Bright Fortune can help with identifying information and communicating about policies arranged through it. The insurer assesses the claim under the policy. An acknowledgement, request for documents or discussion of an event does not itself confirm cover or settlement.

Keep a record of requests and provide updates when material developments arise. If you do not understand a request or decision, ask for an explanation and the relevant policy provision.
