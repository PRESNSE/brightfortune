# Cookies and website storage


## Proposed launch configuration
The initial website is designed without advertising pixels, embedded maps, third-party video players or optional analytics. Images and the website font are served locally. The enquiry form does not automatically subscribe visitors to marketing.

This notice describes the intended configuration. Before public release, the developer and Bright Fortune should confirm the actual behaviour of hosting, security services and any additions to the site.

## Functional use
Technical mechanisms may be needed to deliver pages, protect the enquiry route or remember a choice you make. Their exact names and duration should be recorded if persistent storage is introduced. The default design does not require a marketing-consent banner simply to display local images or use ordinary links.

## Optional services
If analytics, advertising, embedded media or other optional services are later added, this notice and the consent implementation should be reviewed before those services are enabled. Where choices are offered, declining should be as straightforward as accepting and should not prevent access to ordinary website content.

## Browser controls and questions
Your browser may provide controls for stored data and cookies. Changing those settings can affect some website functions. For questions about information handling, read the [privacy notice](/privacy) or use the verified details on the [contact page](/contact).
