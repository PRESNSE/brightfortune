# Privacy notice


## About this notice
This notice describes the proposed handling of enquiries through the Bright Fortune (Far East) Limited website. In the draft website it is displayed for review. Before public operation, Bright Fortune will confirm that it accurately reflects its contact channels, service providers and working practices.

## Information you provide
The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, area of interest and message. You may also provide a telephone number or company name. Required fields are identified. If the required details are not provided, we may be unable to respond to the enquiry through the form.

Please do not include identity numbers, payment details, medical records or detailed claim documents in a general enquiry. We will explain how further information should be provided if it is needed for a requested service.

## How enquiry information is used
Enquiry details are used to consider and respond to your request, contact you about that request and maintain relevant correspondence. The form does not enrol you in marketing communications. A separate notice and any required choice will be provided before using your information for a different purpose.

## Who may handle the information
Authorised Bright Fortune personnel may handle the information needed to respond. Website hosting, security and email service providers may process data to support the enquiry channel. Where your requested insurance service requires information to be given to an insurer or another relevant recipient, the purpose and appropriate process will be explained as part of that service.

The live service-provider arrangements, relevant locations of processing and any associated disclosures must be confirmed before public form activation. The draft does not assert that all information is stored in Hong Kong.

## Retention and protection
Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose and applicable requirements, then securely deleted or otherwise handled under the company's approved retention procedures. Access should be limited to those who need it for their work. The website is not intended as a repository for sensitive policy or claim documents.

## Access, correction and privacy questions
You may ask about the personal data held in relation to your enquiry or request access or correction through the verified contact details on our [contact page](/contact). Address privacy requests to the person responsible for data protection at Bright Fortune (Far East) Limited. The public notice must identify the approved postal/email request channel before form activation.

## Cookies and updates
See our [cookie notice](/cookies) for the website's proposed storage and tracking configuration. This notice should be updated when the actual handling arrangements change. A publication or revision date will appear after approval; no review date is implied by this draft.
