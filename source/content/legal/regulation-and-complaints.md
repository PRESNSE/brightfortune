# Regulation & Complaints


## Company identity
Bright Fortune (Far East) Limited is the company described on this website. Its published website identifies insurance broking services in Hong Kong. Current licensing particulars and authorised business lines must be checked against the Insurance Authority's public register and the company's records before this page is approved for public release.

A verified regulatory statement will appear here after that check. Historical membership wording on an older website should not be taken as confirmation of current licensing particulars. In preview, this paragraph is an explicit review note rather than a regulatory claim.

## If you have a concern
If you are dissatisfied with service provided by Bright Fortune, raise the matter through the verified contact details on our [contact page](/contact) and make clear that it is a complaint. Briefly explain what happened, the relevant policy or enquiry reference and the outcome you are seeking.

Please avoid sending sensitive supporting documents through the public enquiry form. Ask for the appropriate channel if detailed records are needed. Keep copies of the correspondence and any references supplied.

## Reviewing the concern
The proposed process is to acknowledge the concern, identify the relevant information, review the circumstances and explain the response and any next steps. The company will confirm the responsible person, actual procedure and any response time commitments before public release. This draft does not promise a response deadline.

## Further information
If a concern is not resolved, the appropriate external route depends on the nature of the issue and the organisations involved. The public version should identify verified, applicable complaint channels after review. Do not import BMS's UK complaints scheme or imply that a Hong Kong scheme necessarily has jurisdiction over every broker complaint.

Using a complaints process does not automatically suspend policy reporting requirements or other time limits. Refer to the relevant policy and obtain clarification where needed.
