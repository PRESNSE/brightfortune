# Asset usage
All photos are bundled to remove sourcing work from the implementation session. Original JPGs plus responsive WebP derivatives are present. Use local derivative paths from asset-manifest.json, not third-party hotlinks. Preserve attribution/provenance records even where the general licence does not require attribution. No claim is made that model/property/trademark permissions have been individually verified. Company approval is required before public use; the port photograph includes distant vessel branding, so crop or substitute if unsuitable.

Figtree: Google Fonts upstream download, SIL Open Font License in public/fonts/OFL.txt. Keep the licence with distribution. Load locally. The SVG wordmark is a new draft typeset asset, not Bright Fortune's supplied official logo. It can be used for preview; company may approve it or replace it.

Media allocation: home left harbour, centre architecture, right workplace; about harbour/workplace; commercial and property architecture; marine port; life beach; medical stethoscope; claims/contact workplace. Reuse is deliberate and controlled for the small draft. Avoid repeating an image in adjacent sections on the same page where a different allocated asset exists. Meaningful card images use manifest alt; decorative hero crop uses alt="".

Video fallback is the default still hero, not a blank placeholder. Do not download BMS video or fabricate footage ownership. Stock photos are a practical substitute and reduce fidelity to BMS's particular moving human imagery; this is an explicit limitation.
