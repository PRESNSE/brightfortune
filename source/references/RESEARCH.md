The original visual assumptions were not sufficiently close to the observed current homepage. New design files supersede them: white header, centred triptych hero, Figtree 500, navy #213752, orange punctuation, pale neutrals, capsule media and multicolour curved fact blocks. Observed H1 144/149.76px; H2 72/83.52px. Exact responsive breakpoints and animated sequence timing remain designed approximations. The mobile specification is explicit to avoid leaving Claude to invent it, but is not measured BMS mobile CSS.

## Company fact status
Company primary pages support the service list and historic dates used. They are published company statements, not current policy availability or licensing verification. Original marketing phrasing is rewritten. The current authority of every marine subclass, group plan or specialist product has not been established.

The provisional phone/address come from the uploaded blueprint and public broker directories; they require internal confirmation. Search surfaced conflicting linked-long-term classifications in directories, so no licence business-line assertion is approved. Email is deliberately null. The hyphenated bright-fortune.com belongs to a different business and must not be used to fill the gap.

## Assets
Six stock photographs downloaded, viewed as a contact sheet, and assigned by depicted subject. They are not BMS assets. Figtree variable font and OFL licence included. Original provisional wordmark is supplied as SVG. Image release/brand approval remains a launch field. Still photography is a defined draft substitute for BMS video, not a claim that this pack contains equivalent footage.

## Included / absent
Included: 21-route specification, original page copy, five complete draft guides, legal review drafts, machine-readable data, design tokens, editable HTML reference, composition map, local photography/font/wordmark, SEO/UX/server contracts and validation utilities.
Not included: production application source, BMS source/media, genuine staff photographs, confirmed licensing/contact/hosting credentials, tested live enquiry delivery, Chinese translation or completed external listings/backlinks.
